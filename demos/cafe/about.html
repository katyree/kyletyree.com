About the example

A website with room for your story.

Main Street Café is a fictional business. Kyle built this five-page example to show how a café can help customers choose what to eat and plan a visit.

Built around a customer’s visit.

The menu is readable on a phone. Hours have their own page. Contact details are easy to reach.

For your café, this space would feature your real story and photographs of your team, space, and food.

No customer reviews or business history have been invented for this example.
