About the example

A local service business, clearly explained.

Ridgeline Home Services is fictional. Kyle built this five-page example to show how a trades business could turn a visitor’s question into a useful inquiry.

What this website demonstrates

  • A clear service list, with room to explain what is and isn’t offered.
  • A dedicated service-area page.
  • A short estimate form that asks about the actual job.
  • Readable pages and navigation on phones.

For a real business, this page would introduce the owner and show their actual work. This example makes no claims about customers, licenses, reviews, or completed projects.
