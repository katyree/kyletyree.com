Fair questions

Good things to ask.

I already have a Facebook page. Would a website help?

It depends on what your customers need. A website gives you a dedicated place for services, menus, hours, and inquiries that you can link from Facebook, your business listing, and printed materials. I’ll help you decide whether that would be useful for your business.

How much will the whole project cost?

The standard package is $500 setup plus a required $50/month plan from launch. Custom features and approved extras are quoted separately. See the full scope and payment details.

What does the $50 monthly plan cover?

Hosting, one domain costing up to $25/year, website maintenance, and one hour of content edits each month without rollover. The plan is month-to-month. Extra edits cost $50/hour in 15-minute increments, with your approval first.

How long does a website take?

The target is 2–4 weeks after receiving your deposit and required content. We agree on the date before work starts; delayed feedback or added scope can move it.

What do I need to provide?

Provide your business details, services or menu, photos, and logo. I draft and polish the wording from those details. New branding and custom features are quoted separately.

Can you update an existing website?

Send me the link and explain what isn’t working for you. I’ll review whether changes to the current site or a rebuild make sense before proposing work.

Are the example websites real businesses?

No. They are complete demonstration websites for fictional businesses. You can browse every page and try the forms, but those forms do not send or save information.

Can you set up business email?

Yes. Google Workspace setup is $100 for a straightforward new account with up to three users. You own the account and pay Google directly. Existing email migration is quoted separately. Email setup details

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel before your next billing date. Service continues through the paid period, then your new provider can take over your domain and paid-for website files. Before launch, service payments are refundable, excluding approved, nonrefundable third-party purchases. Cancellation and handoff details
